Philosophy

LUVARÉN was founded on a singular conviction: that a suit is not merely clothing — it is an assertion of identity, a silent declaration of character. Born in Jakarta and shaped by the finest tailoring traditions, each piece we create is a testament to the art of meticulous construction.

From the first consultation to the final stitch, every garment passes through the hands of master craftsmen who have dedicated their lives to perfecting the craft. We work with premium fabrics that honour both tradition and the wearer's vision.

This is not fast fashion. This is a covenant between maker and wearer — unhurried, uncompromised, unforgettable.

What We Offer

The LUVARÉN
Service

01

Bespoke Tailoring

Layanan bespoke tailoring premium dengan proses pembuatan pakaian yang sepenuhnya dibuat khusus sesuai ukuran, bentuk tubuh, dan preferensi pelanggan. Setiap detail dikerjakan secara personal mulai dari pemilihan bahan, desain, model kerah, potongan, hingga finishing untuk menghasilkan pakaian yang lebih presisi, nyaman, dan eksklusif. Dikerjakan oleh tailor berpengalaman dengan standar jahitan rapi dan kualitas tinggi untuk menciptakan tampilan yang elegan, profesional, dan berkelas.

Mulai Rp 2.000.000

02

Garment Production

Layanan custom garment production untuk pembuatan kemeja, kaos, seragam, jaket, dan berbagai apparel custom dengan kualitas produksi yang rapi dan profesional. Mendukung kebutuhan brand fashion, perusahaan, komunitas, hingga clothing line dengan pilihan bahan, desain, ukuran, sablon, bordir, dan detail finishing yang dapat disesuaikan. Dikerjakan dengan standar jahitan berkualitas untuk menghasilkan produk yang nyaman, tahan lama, dan memiliki tampilan premium.

Custom Pricing · Consultation

03

Home Service

Layanan home service tailoring dengan pelayanan langsung ke rumah, kantor, maupun lokasi pelanggan untuk konsultasi, pengukuran, dan pemesanan pakaian custom tanpa perlu datang ke workshop. Cocok untuk kebutuhan bespoke tailoring, jas, kemeja, seragam, dan apparel custom dengan pengukuran presisi, pelayanan profesional, serta hasil jahitan premium yang nyaman, rapi, dan eksklusif.

Complimentary with booking

Our Distinction

Why
Choose
LUVARÉN

Because the finest things are never rushed, and the most enduring impressions are made before a word is spoken.

01

Premium Heritage Materials

We work with premium mills and heritage fabrics — from European wools to authentic Indonesian batik — ensuring every cloth carries a legacy worth wearing.

02

Master Craftsmanship

Each piece is constructed by artisans trained in classical hand-tailoring. Every lapel, seam, and buttonhole is executed with deliberate care — some garments require over 80 hours of skilled labour.

03

Completely Personalised Service

From your first fitting to final delivery, you are attended to by a dedicated consultant. We take 22+ individual measurements and revisit every detail until the garment fits like a second skin.

04

Discreet Client Relationship

Our clientele includes executives, public figures, and military officers. We maintain absolute confidentiality and build long-term relationships — not transactions.

05

Lifetime Alterations

A LUVARÉN garment is a lifelong commitment. We provide lifetime alteration services, ensuring your piece continues to fit flawlessly through every season and chapter of your life.
